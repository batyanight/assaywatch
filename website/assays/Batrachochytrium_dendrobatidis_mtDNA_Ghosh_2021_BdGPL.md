## Records reaching the statistics

9,428 records were screened for this assay and **8 (0%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 9,420 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

## What was deposited, by lineage

Lineages here are sequence groups, not species or taxids. Each is a group of records
that look alike over the amplicon and its flanks with the oligo sites masked, cut at
0.05 Mash distance. Records can link one species to the next, so a group
can hold several; a percent in its name is the share of its top species. The groups
come from sequence this assay does not measure, so the mix below can move without the
assay changing. The dashed line on the chart above recomputes each year on the whole
period's mix: where the two lines part, what got sequenced changed, not what the assay
detects.

VERIFIED against Bd mitochondrial records CP161941.1 and CP161942.1. Two details confirm the reading beyond doubt. First, the MGB_BdGPL probe is drawn across a line break in the figure, with a lone CGC boxed at the start of the next line; the joined 21-mer TATAATTGAAGTATGAGGCGC occurs contiguously in the record, which proves both that the two printed lines are contiguous and that the probe spans them. Second, for BdCAPE the 64 bp product recovered from the record matches the figure's printed sequence at every position EXCEPT two - position 31 T-&gt;G and position 36 C-&gt;A - and both of those fall inside the probe footprint while the primers match perfectly.</p><p>THOSE TWO MISMATCHES ARE THE ASSAY WORKING, NOT A TRANSCRIPTION ERROR. They are the lineage-diagnostic sites: the primers are conserved across Bd and only the probe discriminates, so a BdGPL genome carries the GPL allele at both positions and is correctly invisible to the CAPE probe. CP161941 and CP161942 both carry the non-CAPE allele and CP161941 carries the GPL probe site perfectly, which is consistent.</p><p><b>2026-10-04</b> WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE DRIFT METRIC, and it is the sharpest example in the registry. These two assays are DESIGNED NOT TO DETECT most Bd. A detected fraction computed over all Bd records is meaningless for them - a low share is the assay succeeding at discrimination, not failing at detection. They cannot be read with the same threshold as a pan-lineage assay such as Batrachochytrium_dendrobatidis_ITS1_5_8S_Boyle_2004, and they are the concrete case the per-clade estimand discussion in section 3.1 of docs/journal/2026-10-04-pipeline-review.md is about. This entry is the BdGPL probe. Measured on CP161941.1: forward at 117701, reverse at 117770, a 98 bp product with the probe inside it. amplicon_bp is measured; the paper states no size. BdGPL is the panzootic lineage behind global amphibian declines, so a detection here is the finding that matters epidemiologically.
