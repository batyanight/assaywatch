## Mismatch distribution

Each oligonucleotide drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the
scoring penalizes more heavily, and it differs by oligonucleotide: for a primer the
3′ window, where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site; for a probe the
central third, since a hydrolysis probe is never extended — its 3′ end carries the
quencher and it is cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex
stability. Bar height is the proportion of records differing at that position; bar
color is the consequence, measured as the fall in detection between records
differing there and those not:

<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-strong"></span> detection falls by 25 points or more</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-mid"></span> falls 10 to 25</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-weak"></span> little or no effect — variation the assay tolerates</span>

Counts are available on hover. A tall gray bar indicates a tolerated polymorphism; a
short red bar indicates a position of consequence.

## Records reaching the statistics

114 records were screened for this assay and **34 (30%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 80 produced no amplicon at all.

This is material because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

## Curation record

Added 2026-10-04 from Grizzle et al. 

<details class="aw-curation"><summary>The full note, as the curator wrote it · in the order the curator wrote it, oldest first</summary><p><b>2026-10-04</b> Added 2026-10-04 from Grizzle et al. 2003, read from the paper's own Methods, which prints both sequences in the running text. This is the canonical LMBV-specific conventional PCR used by US fish-health laboratories. Product 248 bp, stated in the paper and independently measured here. VERIFIED against the paper's own design sequence: both primers match AF080250 - the accession Grizzle names as the design target - with ZERO mismatches, giving exactly 248 bp. That is the cleanest confirmation of a transcription this registry has, since the paper tells you which record it designed against and the primers land on it perfectly.</p><p>SCOPE IS DELIBERATELY NARROWER THAN THE SPECIES, because the paper's claim is. Grizzle chose these primers to match 'a portion of the LMBV genome that was distinct from the sequence of doctorfish virus and guppy virus', and Fig. 1 shows they do not amplify either, nor frog virus 3, nor EHNV, nor red seabream iridovirus - even though Mao et al. 1999 had concluded doctorfish and guppy viruses are the same species as LMBV. So non-detection of doctorfish or guppy virus is this assay working as designed, and they are excluded from the denominator: the query covers the bass and Santee-Cooper nodes only (114 records) and leaves out doctorfish, guppy and guppyfish iridovirus (9 records). Screening confirms the exclusion is real rather than nominal: doctorfish and guppy virus each carry 6 mismatches across the pair. Contrast LMBV_dsDNA_MCP_RPA_Li_2025, which detects both and is therefore a group-level assay, not an LMBV-specific one - the two entries answer different questions and should not be compared head to head without that in mind.</p><p>POSSIBLE REAL DRIFT, and the reason this entry is worth watching. Against the 1990s US sequence it was designed on, the fit is perfect. Against contemporary LMBV genomes it is not: PX879566.1 (2025) carries 4 forward and 1 reverse mismatches, and PV833272.1 (2024) has no 248 bp site at all within the search budget. That is the same order of divergence as the doctorfish and guppy viruses this assay was built to exclude, which would mean its discrimination has eroded from both sides. Treat as a hypothesis until the pipeline has run this group properly - the numbers above come from four hand-checked genomes, not from a screened archive. Note Grizzle also ran a beta-actin internal control (632 bp, L36342) alongside; host-gene controls are out of scope for this registry and are not entered.</p></details>
