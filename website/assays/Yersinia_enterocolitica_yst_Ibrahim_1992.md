third: a hydrolysis probe is never extended, its 3′ end carries the quencher and it is

## Scope

**Meant to detect:** Pathogenic Yersinia enterocolitica, yst (heat-stable enterotoxin) gene.  
**Scored against:** every record retrieved.  
**Retrieved from:** Yersinia enterocolitica (taxid 630), limited by a query on the title or gene.

This entry declares no scope restriction, so every record retrieved for its target taxid counts toward the denominator. Where an assay was designed for a narrower range than its taxid covers, that presents as low coverage rather than drift; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).

Retrieval query:

```
(yst[Gene] OR ystA[Gene] OR "heat-stable enterotoxin"[Title] OR yst[Title]) AND 1:30000[SLEN] NOT "whole genome shotgun"[Title]
```

A retrieval query is a claim about which records the assay is tested against, and it can stop being accurate without producing an error; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).

## Records reaching the statistics

38 records were screened for this assay and **37 (97%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 1 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

## What was deposited, by lineage

Lineages here are sequence groups, not species or taxids. Each is a group of records
that look alike over the amplicon and its flanks with the oligo sites masked, cut at
0.05 Mash distance. Records can link one species to the next, so a group
can hold several; a percent in its name is the share of its top species. The groups
come from sequence this assay does not measure, so the mix below can move without the
assay changing. The dashed line on the chart above recomputes each year on the whole
period's mix: where the two lines part, what got sequenced changed, not what the assay
detects.

Corrected to the paper's base.\nWHAT THE CORRECTION ACTUALLY DID, which is more interesting than a typo. Screened against the 35 Y. enterocolitica assemblies both ways, the result inverts: with the stored G, 13 of 35 records match perfectly and 22 carry a mismatch; with the paper's A, 22 match perfectly and 13 carry one. So this position is genuinely polymorphic in the target - both bases circulate - and the paper's primer sits on the majority allele while the stored variant sat on the minority one. An R at that position would match all 35, but that would be designing a new primer rather than recording a published one, so it is noted and not done.\nThe error was SILENT to the drift metric: a single mismatch 9 bases from the 3' end costs exactly 1.0, and detect_max is 1.0, so all 35 records read `detect` either way. The tool cannot catch its own input errors; only checking against the paper can. It was not harmless, though - it spent the entire detect budget, so any second mismatch anywhere would have tipped a record to `reduced` for a reason that did not exist.\nTWO DISCREPANCIES REMAIN UNRESOLVED and are deliberately not 'fixed', because unlike the base above they cannot be settled by reading the table:\n 1. The forward primer. Stored YE-1 is CTGTCTTCATTTGGAGCATTC; the paper's yst-Pr2a is AATGCTGTCTTCATTTGGAGC. They share a 17 nt core but the stored one is shifted 4 bases 3'-ward, losing AATG from the 5' end and gaining ATTC at the 3' end. A 3'-end change is exactly what matters most for extension, so this is not a cosmetic difference. It could be a deliberate redesign or a transcription slip, and rewriting it on a guess would be worse than flagging it.\n 2. amplicon_bp is 159, but the paper's positions give 92 - 30 + 1 = 63 bp. Nothing in the paper states 159. With the stored forward primer the span would be about 59 bp. Where 159 came from is unknown.\nAlso recorded: the paper names these yst-Pr2a / yst-Pr2b, not YE-1 / YE-2, and the target is the ystA enterotoxin reading frame. YE-1/YE-2 are the names first entered. CORRECTED 2026-11-10: forward corrected to the printed yst-Pr2a (Table 3 positions 30-50; Fig. 1); the stored form had been shifted 4 nt 3'-ward along Fig. 1. The printed primer is exact on all 37 products, giving 163 bp, which the paper states (p1, p3); 159 was the shifted primer's product, and the earlier '63 bp' came from the text layer reading the superscript 192 as 92. Table 3 prints the position order, so orientation is stated. Two Y. kristensenii strains gave a product of the same size (p3).</p></details>
