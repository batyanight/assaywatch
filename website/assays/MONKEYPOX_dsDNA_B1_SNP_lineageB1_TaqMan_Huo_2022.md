## By lineage

Every evaluable record is one lineage, **Orthopoxvirus monkeypox (3431483)** (1,676 of 4,794 detected, 35.0%, 95% interval 33.6–36.3%), so splitting by lineage adds nothing to the figure above. The split matters where an assay spans several clades: a pooled percentage can look healthy while one lineage fails completely.


## Scope — what this assay is judged against

This entry declares no `scope_taxids`, so every record retrieved for its target taxid counts toward the denominator. Where an assay was designed for a narrower set than its taxid covers, that reads as low coverage rather than as drift — see [scoping](../scoping.md).

Disclosures on this entry: `amplicon_measured_not_published`, `scope_narrower_than_taxid`.


## What reached the statistics

14,204 records were screened for this assay and **13,208 (93%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 996 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one drifted primer counts as a failure while a record with both drifted leaves the denominator entirely. The bias is known, measured and not obviously fixable — see [known limitations](../what-the-output-is-worth.md).
