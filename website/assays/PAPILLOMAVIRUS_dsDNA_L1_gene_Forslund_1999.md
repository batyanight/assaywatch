## The verdict, and the numbers behind it

**declining** — below the threshold, measurably lower than a baseline that once cleared it

Everything the call was made from, so you can check it yourself:

| | detected | evaluable | share | 95% interval |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| recent window (24 months, from Oct 2024) | 1,195 | 1,591 | 75.1% | 72.9% – 77.2% |
| baseline (everything earlier) | 8,263 | 14,684 | 56.3% | 55.5% – 57.1% |

Change from baseline, Newcombe interval: **16.5% to 21.1%**. Threshold: **90.0%**.

*pooled share 75.1% has low coverage, but lineage Alphapapillomavirus 9 (337041) (320 records) detects only 12.8% (9.6%-16.9%), down from 43.7% on 9521 earlier records; lineage verdict overrides to declining*

Trend across deposit years: by record rising (p=1.75e-25, 43 years); by footprint falling (p=0.000616); **the two disagree on direction**. Shown for context; it does not feed the verdict.

From the 2026-10-31 run. A status tells you where to look rather than what is true — [what the output is worth](../what-the-output-is-worth.md) explains how often these calls are right.
