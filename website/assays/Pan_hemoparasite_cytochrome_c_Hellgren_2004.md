## Mismatch distribution

Each oligonucleotide drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the
scoring penalizes more heavily, and it differs by oligonucleotide: for a primer the
3′ window, where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site; for a probe the
central third, since a hydrolysis probe is never extended — its 3′ end carries the
quencher and it is cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex
stability. Bar height is the proportion of records differing at that position; bar
color is the consequence, measured as the fall in detection between records
differing there and those not:

<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-strong"></span> detection falls by 25 points or more</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-mid"></span> falls 10 to 25</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-weak"></span> little or no effect — variation the assay tolerates</span>

Counts are available on hover. A tall gray bar indicates a tolerated polymorphism; a
short red bar indicates a position of consequence.

## By lineage

of deposits can be 100% of the cases in one region — so a pooled percentage can look
healthy while one clade fails completely. Worst first. Lineages with too few records
to say anything are left out, and a lineage whose whole interval sits below 90% is marked.

## Records reaching the statistics

18,980 records were screened for this assay and **1,023 (5%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 17,957 produced no amplicon at all.

This is material because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

## Curation record

AMPLICON SIZE IS SUSPECT. The stored 617 bp is the product of HaemNF/HaemNR2, which the paper attributes to Waldenstrom et al. 2004 - a DIFFERENT first-round pair. For HaemNFI/HaemNR3 the paper states no size. 617 should not be trusted until it is measured on real records. | SCOPE 2026-10-19: scope_taxids is the avian haemosporidia the design paper names in its title, 'A new PCR assay for simultaneous studies of Leucocytozoon, Plasmodium, and Haemoproteus from avian blood': the five avian Plasmodium subgenera in NCBI (Haemamoeba 418104, Huffia 418105, Novyella 418110, Giovannolaia 418112, Bennettinia 419353; every species NCBI places under them is a bird parasite, checked 2026-10-19), Haemoproteus 77521, Parahaemoproteus 587283 and Leucocytozoon 195944. Before this the assay was judged against all Haemosporida, and its records were mostly mammalian: on the 2026-10-17 calls, 2,240 Laverania (0% detected) and 1,618 subgenus Plasmodium (11%) against about 130 in the avian subgenera (nearly all detected). Deliberately out of scope: Plasmodium records with no subgenus (e.g. 31272 'Plasmodium sp.', 783 records at 73%), because NCBI does not say their host; some are avian lineages, and that is a known loss.
