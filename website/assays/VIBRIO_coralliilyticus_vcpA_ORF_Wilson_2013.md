## Mismatch distribution

Each oligonucleotide drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the
scoring penalizes more heavily, and it differs by oligonucleotide: for a primer the
3′ window, where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site; for a probe the
central third, since a hydrolysis probe is never extended — its 3′ end carries the
quencher and it is cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex
stability. Bar height is the proportion of records differing at that position; bar
color is the consequence, measured as the fall in detection between records
differing there and those not:

<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-strong"></span> detection falls by 25 points or more</span>

<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-weak"></span> little or no effect — variation the assay tolerates</span>

short red bar indicates a position of consequence.

## Scope

This entry declares no scope restriction, so every record retrieved for its target taxid counts toward the denominator. Where an assay was designed for a narrower range than its taxid covers, that presents as low coverage rather than drift; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).

Retrieval query:

```
vcpA[All Fields] OR vtpA[All Fields] OR "complete genome"[Title] OR "chromosome"[Title]
```

A retrieval query is a claim about the population against which the assay is evaluated, and it can cease to be accurate without producing an error; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).

Disclosures on this entry: `amplicon_disagrees_with_paper`, `oligos_from_secondary_source`.


## Records reaching the statistics

97 records were screened for this assay and **35 (36%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 62 produced no amplicon at all.

This is material because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

The paper's other method, the dnaJ TaqMan assay of Pollock et al. 2010, is a different paper and a different target; see the notes of the dnaJ entry or the lead's report. MEASURED on the complete NCBI Datasets census of Vibrio coralliilyticus (txid190893), downloaded 2026-10-06: 101 assembly accessions = 54 distinct assemblies once the paired GenBank (GCA) and RefSeq (GCF) copies are collapsed (14 complete genome, 4 chromosome, 8 scaffold, 28 contig level; 7,604 sequences), plus the 16 complete vcpA/vtpA CDS records in GenBank (JQ345033-JQ345046 from this paper, GQ452012, MH794510 VtpA; each 1,824 bp) and 2 partial vcpA records (OL321784/OL321785). Assemblies were screened as a whole; the unit below is the distinct assembly, not the contig.</p><p>THIS ENTRY: vcpAF (5'-ATGAAACAACGTCAAATGCTTTG-3') and vcpAR (5'-CCCTTTCACTTCCGATGTTGTG-3'), the conventional PCR that amplifies the whole vcpA ORF for sequencing and phylogeny, stated by the paper as 1.8 kb (single amplicon in all global isolates except strain P3, LMG 23695, which did not amplify).</p><p>THE PAPER DID NOT DESIGN THESE PRIMERS: it says they 'were previously designed to amplify the entire vcpA open reading frame (ORF) of V. coralliilyticus strain P1 and subsequently used to compare the vcpA genes from geographically disparate isolates', citing its reference 18, Pollock FJ, Wilson B, Johnson WR, Morris PJ, Willis BL, Bourne DG (2010) Phylogeny of the coral pathogen Vibrio coralliilyticus, Environ Microbiol Rep 2:172-178. oligos_designed_here is therefore false; that paper's DOI was not read and no DOI is entered for it. vcpAF is the first 23 nt of the ORF (exact in all 16 complete CDS records). vcpAR is the same reverse site as vcpARTR, about 30 nt past the stop codon, so the product runs ORF plus about 30 nt of 3' flank. RESULT: 53 of 54 distinct assemblies give a product, all 'detect': 1,854 bp in 38 assemblies (the mode), 1,853 in 9, 1,855 in 2, 1,851 in 3 and 1,852 in 1. The paper's '1.8 kb' is consistent; the exact length is ORF 1,824 bp plus flank. EVERY detect call carries one standing reverse-primer mismatch (penalty 0.5, 12 nt from the primer's 3' end): vcpAR has C where all the genomes screened have A, whereas the overlapping vcpARTR of the same paper matches exactly. Whether this is a typo in the printed primer, a P1-specific base or a true design choice cannot be told from here, and a single internal mismatch is unlikely to stop the 30-cycle 64 C PCR the paper ran; the primer was entered as printed. 1 assembly fails: SCSIO 43001 (1,840 bp, forward primer mismatch at its 3'-terminal base, reverse 4 mismatches), the same strain that fails the SYBR pair (its closest vcpA-like sequence shares only about 12% of the P1 vcpA 25-mers sampled, against 66-73% in OCN008 and Rb102; an in-silico observation only). The 16 complete CDS records give no_amplicon (truncated) because they end at the stop codon, before the reverse site. SCOPE: gene-level PCR for typing; as for the SYBR pair, a vcpA detection, not a species identification. Strain P3 (LMG 23695), which the paper reports as non-amplifying, was not identified among the records screened, so that wet failure is not explained here.</p></details>
