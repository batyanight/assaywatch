Each oligo drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the scoring
penalizes more heavily, and it depends on the oligo. For a primer it is the 3′ window,
where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site. For a probe it is the central
third: a hydrolysis probe is never extended, its 3′ end carries the quencher and it is
cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex stability. Bar height is the
share of records that differ at that position. Bar color is what that costs, measured
as the fall in detection between records that differ there and records that do not:

**Meant to detect:** The genus Plasmodium, by a genus-conserved primer pair.  
**Scored against:** every record retrieved.  
**Retrieved from:** Plasmodium (taxid 5820), limited by a query on the title or gene.

This entry declares no scope restriction, so every record retrieved for its target taxid counts toward the denominator. Where an assay was designed for a narrower range than its taxid covers, that presents as low coverage rather than drift; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).

## What was deposited, by lineage

Lineages here are sequence groups, not species or taxids. Each is a group of records
that look alike over the amplicon and its flanks with the oligo sites masked, cut at
0.05 Mash distance. Records can link one species to the next, so a group
can hold several; a percent in its name is the share of its top species. The groups
come from sequence this assay does not measure, so the mix below can move without the
assay changing. The dashed line on the chart above recomputes each year on the whole
period's mix: where the two lines part, what got sequenced changed, not what the assay
detects.

*102 of 734 records unplaced (14%); 15 of 734 diverged, with no site to cluster on.*

Grim et al. 2003 (10.1638/02-070) used them and cited "as described previously [ref 23 = Li et al. 1995]". The entry was first cited to Grim 2003.</p><p><b>2026-11-10</b> NEST CHECKED AND ANSWERED 2026-11-10 (ROADMAP, "The nested malaria assay"): Li 1995 describes NO nest, so `nested_family` and `nested_round` stay null, and this entry is unrelated to the Hellgren 2004 haemosporidian nest it was grouped with by the question. Evidence, from the paper read page by page. (a) One round. Methods: cDNA is primed with the antisense primer, then "further amplification of the cDNA was accomplished by routine PCR" at "a total of 30 cycles", and nothing is carried into a second reaction. (b) Table I's eleven oligos are one pair plus probes, not two pairs in series: 841/844 (genus, this entry), 842 (genus, sense, internal), 741 and 743 (P. vivax A-gene and S-gene), and 933, 934, 935, 936 (P. ovale, P. malariae, P. falciparum, P. vivax). The species oligos are hybridisation probes, used on Southern transfers of the one gel (Fig. 2b, Fig. 4b and 4c), never as primers. (c) The 566/570 pair is genuinely outside 841/844 on the gene, which is what makes the nest question reasonable, but the paper uses them as ALTERNATIVES for different jobs, not in series: 566/570 "amplifies almost the entire Plasmodium SSU rRNA gene" and had been used for cloning, while "For the purpose of species identification and taxonomy, however, shorter regions of sequence are sufficient. The genus-conserved primer pair 841-844 (Table I) was selected for the work described here". No text runs one into the other.</p><p><b>2026-11-01</b> TWO FINDINGS FOR THE MAINTAINER, FOUND BY THAT READ AND NOT ACTED ON. (1) A PROBE EXISTS AND IS NOT STORED.
