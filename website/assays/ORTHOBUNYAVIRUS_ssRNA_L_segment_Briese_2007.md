<details class="aw-curation"><summary>The full note, as the curator wrote it · in the order the curator wrote it, oldest first</summary><p><b>2026-10-04</b> Added 2026-10-04 from Briese et al. 2007 Table 1 (doi:10.1007/s00705-007-1069-z), read from the paper itself rather than from any secondary list. The table gives each primer's sequence and the genome position of its 3'-terminal base, numbered on the Bunyamwera virus prototype 1943 reference (D00353 = S, M11852 = M, X14383 = L). Degenerate positions are printed as lower-case IUPAC codes and are transcribed here in upper case; sequences are printed in triplets, which is how each primer's length was checked. Non-authentic (cloning-tail) bases are marked in the table by a distinct italic font, and NONE of the primers in this entry carries one except where tail_5prime is set below. amplicon_bp is COMPUTED, not quoted: the paper gives 3' positions, not product sizes, so the product is taken as the span from the forward primer's 5' base to the reverse primer's 5' base using the printed 3' positions and the primer lengths. BUNL-5n carries a 6 nt italic tail (CGCCGC); its 15 nt of authentic sequence has its 3' base at 15, so it binds 1-15, the L-segment 5' terminus. BUNL650R is 30 nt with its 3' base at 601, so it binds 601-630; the product is 1-630 = 630 bp. The arithmetic is corroborated by the primer names, which encode their own positions (BUNS-3-L947 at 947, BUNL650R at 601-630). Treat the size as approximate: it is exact only on the Bunyamwera reference, and these are consensus primers meant for a whole serogroup. SCOPE. The paper designed these from an alignment of 'orthobunyaviruses of the CE and BUN serogroups and Guaroa virus', so the claim is the California encephalitis and Bunyamwera serogroups, not the whole genus. The registry target is genus Orthobunyavirus (~18 serogroups), so a low detected share here is very likely scope rather than drift. Screening the two assays already in the registry on 2026-10-04 showed the pattern plainly: strong on the design serogroups (Cache Valley 14/15, Maguari 4/5) and near-zero on Simbu-serogroup viruses (Oropouche 0/24, Akabane 0/7, Schmallenberg 0/7, Manzanilla 0/4). Left unscoped pending the registry-wide decision on lineage scoping. The L segment was not covered by the registry before this entry. Its own retrieval query, so it fetches a separate archive from the S/M assays. BUNL-5n's authentic part is 15 nt, exactly at the oligo floor.</p></details>
