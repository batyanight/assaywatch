## Records reaching the statistics

4,328 records were screened for this assay and **3,540 (82%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 788 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

## What was deposited, by lineage

Lineages here are sequence groups, not species or taxids. Each is a group of records
that look alike over the amplicon and its flanks with the oligo sites masked, cut at
0.05 Mash distance. Records can link one species to the next, so a group
can hold several; a percent in its name is the share of its top species. The groups
come from sequence this assay does not measure, so the mix below can move without the
assay changing. The dashed line on the chart above recomputes each year on the whole
period's mix: where the two lines part, what got sequenced changed, not what the assay
detects.

Nobody should assume this paper designed them.</p><p><b>2026-10-06</b> THE PAPER'S ROLE FOR THE PAIR: it is the MTBC-level screen (IS6110 PCR) run on 1,142 tissue samples, with M. microti then identified by gyrB PCR-RFLP, so this entry is an MTBC-wide assay, not a M. microti-specific one; the paper itself calls the IS6110 target 'present in multiple copies in the genome of M. microti' and attributes the IS6110/gyrB discrepancy to the sensitivities of the two methods. MEASURED against 596 GenBank MTBC-related records fetched 2026-10-06: a strided sample (every 8th) of the 1,910 short (100 bp-10 kb) txid77643 records matching IS6110[All Fields] or IS6110[Title] (239), all 97 short 'direct repeat'/'DR region'/spoligotyp* records, every third of the 678 M. microti (txid1806) records of 100 bp-100 kb (226), and 46 complete genomes (all M. microti, every 6th M. bovis, every 70th M. tuberculosis, plus other MTBC and non-MTBC genomes). 205 records give a product (172 short records and 33 genomes) with zero mismatches in both primers apart from one 0.5 penalty: 202 are exactly 209 bp, which is the paper's figure (this pair needs no correction, unlike the other size claims checked today), 2 are 210 bp (CHYH01000065, CPII01000077) and one is reported at 1,487 bp (FO203507, M. canettii; one of its two IS6110 copies pairs Ext-1 and Int-1 at 209 bp and the other pairs at 1,487 bp, and the tool keeps one product per record). Three further 'fail' calls (743, 1,383 and 451 bp: AH008262, AH008265, FO203510) are spurious long products from single-primer partial matches in IS6110 flank sequences, not the target. COVERAGE: of the 552 short records, 370 contain neither primer-site flank (IS6110 flank fragments, 23S rRNA and other loci of 200-600 bp, e.g. U24533, X95145) and 8 are 200-245 bp records that end inside the amplicon; none of the short records that contain both sites fails to amplify (no_amplicon with both flanks present: 0). GENOMES: 36 MTBC genome records (RefSeq NZ_ copies included): 33 detect, 3 give no product and carry neither primer site at all (AP024458 M. tuberculosis DN101, and M. canettii CIPT 140070010 and CIPT 140070017, which lack IS6110 copies; confirmed by exact string search for both primers). All 8 non-MTBC genomes in the set (6 M. kansasii records, 2 M. vaccae) give no product, so the pair does not cross-amplify those. IS6110 copy number varies from 1 (most M. bovis, BCG Pasteur 1173P2) to over a dozen (many M. tuberculosis), so the pipeline's detect share is a statement about presence of the element, and IS6110-less MTBC strains (some M. canettii, rare M. tuberculosis) are not detected by design.</p><p>NOT ENTERED FROM THIS PAPER: the second primer pair, IM_dir (5'-CTACTACGCTCAACGCCAG-3') and IM_rev (5'-ATCGACTGCGACAACATCC-3'), described as amplifying the DR region 3' of IS6110 to give 573 or 387 bp for sequencing. It is a genotyping/sequencing primer pair, not a detection assay, and it could not be reproduced on real records: IM_dir matches exactly inside every IS6110 copy and IM_rev matches exactly once, in a spacer at the end of the DR locus, in most MTBC genomes (checked in detail on the M. microti genome CP010333, where the IS6110 inserted in the DR locus has its IM_dir site and the IM_rev spacer both pointing towards lower coordinates, so the two primers cannot pair), but in CP010333 and in the 35 other MTBC genome records no IM_dir site and IM_rev site face each other within 3 kb (the best approximate pair in CP010333 is 844 bp with 5 reverse-primer mismatches), and no product near 387 or 573 bp exists in any genome or short record screened. It is held back as unresolved, not as wrong; the wild-boar isolates' own DR sequences carry no accession in the paper. The registry's existing mycobacterial entries (IS1081, helicase MTC-HELI, genus 16S; Hermes 2018) target other loci, so there is no overlap.</p></details>
