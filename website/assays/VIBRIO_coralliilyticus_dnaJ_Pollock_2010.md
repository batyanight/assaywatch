## Mismatch distribution

Each oligonucleotide drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the
scoring penalizes more heavily, and it differs by oligonucleotide: for a primer the
3′ window, where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site; for a probe the
central third, since a hydrolysis probe is never extended — its 3′ end carries the
quencher and it is cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex
stability. Bar height is the proportion of records differing at that position; bar
color is the consequence, measured as the fall in detection between records
differing there and those not:

<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-strong"></span> detection falls by 25 points or more</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-mid"></span> falls 10 to 25</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-weak"></span> little or no effect — variation the assay tolerates</span>

Counts are available on hover. A tall gray bar indicates a tolerated polymorphism; a
short red bar indicates a position of consequence.

## Scope

This entry declares no scope restriction, so every record retrieved for its target taxid counts toward the denominator. Where an assay was designed for a narrower range than its taxid covers, that presents as low coverage rather than drift; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).

Retrieval query:

```
dnaJ[All Fields] OR "complete genome"[Title] OR "chromosome"[Title]
```

A retrieval query is a claim about the population against which the assay is evaluated, and it can cease to be accurate without producing an error; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).

Disclosures on this entry: `primer_site_polymorphic`.


## Records reaching the statistics

139 records were screened for this assay and **71 (51%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 68 produced no amplicon at all.

This is material because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

The paper (Pollock et al., Appl Environ Microbiol 2010) designed all three oligos: forward Vc_dnaJ_F1 5'-CGGTTCGYGGTGTTTCAAAA-3' (Y = C/T), reverse Vc_dnaJ_R1 5'-AACCTGACCATGACCGTGACA-3', and TaqMan probe Vc_dnaJ_TMP 5'-6-FAM-CAGTGGCGCGAAG-MGBNFQ-3' (13 nt, minor-groove-binder non-fluorescent quencher; the candidate extractor's 'AMCAGTGGCGCGAAGMGBN' is an artefact of the printed label).</p><p><b>2026-10-06</b> THE PAPER'S POSITIONS VERIFIED: it states a 128 bp region, nucleotides 363-490 of dnaJ, chosen for SNPs conserved within V. coralliilyticus. On GenBank AB263028.1 (V. coralliilyticus dnaJ partial cds, strain GTC 2777, 517 bp) F1 sits at 363-382 and the reverse complement of R1 at 470-490, exactly the paper's coordinates and 128 bp, with the probe at 427-439 between them. The paper's own 13 deposits HM215568-HM215580 (398 bp each) end before the R1 site, so only F1 can be checked on them: F1 is at 282-301 in all 13 and matches exactly in 12; strain C2 (HM215569, the strain the paper reports as failing to amplify) has one base different at position 2 of F1 (CCGTTCG for CGGTTCG), which would not by itself explain a wet failure, so the cause lies elsewhere, probably in the part of the amplicon those records do not cover. MEASURED on the complete NCBI Datasets census of V. coralliilyticus (txid190893) downloaded 2026-10-06: 101 assembly accessions = 54 distinct assemblies once GenBank/RefSeq pairs are collapsed (14 complete genome, 4 chromosome, 8 scaffold, 28 contig level). Every one of the 54 gives a 128 bp product (the paper's figure): 51 'detect', 3 'reduced' (RE90, SCSIO 43001, Rb102, each with one or two mismatches in a primer), and the best product per assembly is the unit (other contigs of the same assembly carry dnaJ paralogs that give partial-match 'fail' products and are not the target). Detected share among assemblies: 51/54, with all 54 amplifying.</p><p>THE PROBE IS THE PROBLEM. Counting the 13-mer by exact search inside each best product (the screen cannot, see below): exact in 37 of 54 assemblies, one mismatch in 2 (strains 58 and SCSIO 43001), two mismatches in 14 (variant CAGCGGTGCGAAG; e.g. S2052, S2043, RE22 x2, AIC-5, AIC-7, C154, 071316B, H1, G2-37, SCP4, S3W2-15 and RE90) and three in 1 (Rb102, TAGCGGTGCGAAG). So about a quarter of V. coralliilyticus genomes carry a probe site with two internal mismatches that a 13-mer MGB probe is unlikely to tolerate, while the primers still match.</p><p>THE SCREEN WITH PROBE: among the 54 distinct assemblies, 37 detect, 5 reduced (with 1-2 mismatches per oligo), 12 fail (mostly due to probe mismatches). The paper reports detection of 12 of 13 coral strains and gives no specificity data by dnaJ allele. SCOPE: a species-level assay by design (SNPs conserved within V. coralliilyticus), reported by the paper to miss one Caribbean strain (C2) and to give late non-specific Ct values with five other Vibrio and a Shewanella; cross-reaction was not re-tested here.</p></details>
