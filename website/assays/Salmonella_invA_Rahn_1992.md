## Mismatch distribution

Each oligonucleotide drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the
scoring penalizes more heavily, and it differs by oligonucleotide: for a primer the
3′ window, where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site; for a probe the
central third, since a hydrolysis probe is never extended — its 3′ end carries the
quencher and it is cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex
stability. Bar height is the proportion of records differing at that position; bar
color is the consequence, measured as the fall in detection between records
differing there and those not:

<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-strong"></span> detection falls by 25 points or more</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-mid"></span> falls 10 to 25</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-weak"></span> little or no effect — variation the assay tolerates</span>

Counts are available on hover. A tall gray bar indicates a tolerated polymorphism; a
short red bar indicates a position of consequence.

## Curation record

OLIGOS ARE NOT RAHN'S PUBLISHED PAIR. 

<details class="aw-curation"><summary>The full note, as the curator wrote it · in the order the curator wrote it, oldest first</summary><p><b>2026-10-20</b> OLIGOS ARE NOT RAHN'S PUBLISHED PAIR. Read 2026-10-20 from the local PDF, which has a text layer. Rahn's Table 3 prints exactly two primers, both in lower case: 139 gtgaaattatcgccacgttcgggcaa at invA 287-312, and 141 tcatcgcaccgtcaaaggaacc at 571-550. This entry's Sal-3 is the 3'-terminal 19 nt of 139, missing GTGAAAT from the 5' end; Sal-4 is the 3'-terminal 19 nt of 141, missing TCA. The 3' ends are identical, so the binding sites are the same and the truncation can only lower the penalty an oligo can accumulate, which makes this entry a MORE permissive assay than the paper's. The sizes agree with that reading rather than with the paper: Table 3's locations give 285 bp and the text says "the specific 284 bp product", while 285 minus the 7 and 3 dropped bases is 275, and the 2026-10-17 calls measure 275 bp in 467 of 468 detected or reduced records. So amplicon_bp: 275 is correct FOR THE OLIGOS AS STORED and wrong for the citation. Not changed here: substituting 139/141 changes every call for this assay and is a maintainer's decision, the same way the Chlamydia genus 16S primer was kept as printed on 2026-10-17. Note that the validation case backing this entry (Malorny 2003, 99.6 percent inclusivity on 364 strains) validated 139/141, not this pair. Why it was missed until now: the paper has been on disk all along, but scripts/verify_oligos_against_paper.py reported no-full-text, "no local PDF; needs the PDF". Two independent reasons, both in that script. (1) The papers/ naming convention maps /, ( and ) all to _, and the reverse mapping only undoes /, so this file's stem reconstructs as 10.1016/0890-8508/92/90002-f, which still fullmatches the DOI pattern and so indexes the paper under a DOI nothing asks for while also marking it "named" and barring it from the surname-and-year fallback. Ten PDFs on disk, covering 20 registry entries, have parenthesised DOIs and are unreachable this way, Meyer 1997 ATI and Lozano 1997 among them. (2) printed_oligos requires at least 60 percent upper case, on the stated premise that "papers set sequences in capitals"; Rahn sets both primers entirely in lower case, so it extracts 0 oligos from this text even though both sequences are present in it.</p></details>
