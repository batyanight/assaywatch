## What was deposited, by lineage

Lineages here are sequence groups, not species or taxids. Each is a group of records
that look alike over the amplicon and its flanks with the oligo sites masked, cut at
0.05 Mash distance. Records can link one species to the next, so a group

Degenerate positions are printed as lower-case IUPAC codes and are transcribed here in upper case; sequences are printed in triplets, which is how each primer's length was checked. BUN-M-IXf is 20 nt with its 3' base at 3247, so it binds 3228-3247; BUN-M-XIIr is 20 nt with its 3' base at 3543, so it binds 3543-3562; the product is 3228-3562 = 335 bp.
