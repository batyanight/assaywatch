**Influenza A virus — HA (hemagglutinin), H3 (Shu 2026)**

One-step RT-qPCR with a hydrolysis probe (ZEN/Iowa Black FQ) for the hemagglutinin H3 subtype in the CDC influenza A subtyping panel, cleared in 2008. Built to tell A(H3N2) from A(H1N1)pdm09 in respiratory specimens.
