## Mismatch distribution

Each oligonucleotide drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the
scoring penalizes more heavily, and it differs by oligonucleotide: for a primer the
3′ window, where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site; for a probe the
central third, since a hydrolysis probe is never extended — its 3′ end carries the
quencher and it is cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex
stability. Bar height is the proportion of records differing at that position; bar
color is the consequence, measured as the fall in detection between records
differing there and those not:

<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-strong"></span> detection falls by 25 points or more</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-mid"></span> falls 10 to 25</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-weak"></span> little or no effect — variation the assay tolerates</span>

Counts are available on hover. A tall gray bar indicates a tolerated polymorphism; a
short red bar indicates a position of consequence.

Text extraction returns nothing, so the page was rendered and read, the boxes transcribed, and the reverse primers reverse-complemented off the bottom strand by hand. That is exactly the kind of transcription this registry has been burnt by before, so none of it was trusted until it was checked against real sequence. VERIFIED against Bd mitochondrial records CP161941.1 and CP161942.1. Two details confirm the reading beyond doubt. First, the MGB_BdGPL probe is drawn across a line break in the figure, with a lone CGC boxed at the start of the next line; the joined 21-mer TATAATTGAAGTATGAGGCGC occurs contiguously in the record, which proves both that the two printed lines are contiguous and that the probe spans them. Second, for BdCAPE the 64 bp product recovered from the record matches the figure's printed sequence at every position EXCEPT two - position 31 T-&gt;G and position 36 C-&gt;A - and both of those fall inside the probe footprint while the primers match perfectly.</p><p>THOSE TWO MISMATCHES ARE THE ASSAY WORKING, NOT A TRANSCRIPTION ERROR. They are the lineage-diagnostic sites: the primers are conserved across Bd and only the probe discriminates, so a BdGPL genome carries the GPL allele at both positions and is correctly invisible to the CAPE probe. CP161941 and CP161942 both carry the non-CAPE allele and CP161941 carries the GPL probe site perfectly, which is consistent.</p><p><b>2026-10-04</b> WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE DRIFT METRIC, and it is the sharpest example in the registry. These two assays are DESIGNED NOT TO DETECT most Bd. A detected fraction computed over all Bd records is meaningless for them - a low share is the assay succeeding at discrimination, not failing at detection. They cannot be read with the same threshold as a pan-lineage assay such as CHYTRID_Bd_Internal_transcribes_spa_Boyle_2004, and they are the concrete case the per-clade estimand discussion in section 3.1 of docs/journal/2026-10-04-pipeline-review.md is about. This entry is the BdGPL probe. Measured on CP161941.1: forward at 117701, reverse at 117770, a 98 bp product with the probe inside it. amplicon_bp is measured; the paper states no size. BdGPL is the panzootic lineage behind global amphibian declines, so a detection here is the finding that matters epidemiologically. Its sibling is CHYTRID_Bd_mtDNA_lineage_BdCAPE_Ghosh_2021.</p><p><b>2026-10-05</b> SECOND INDEPENDENT READING, 2026-10-05, by a different session that did not look at this entry's stored sequences until afterwards. Figure 1 was re-rendered at 230 dpi and all six oligos of the two Ghosh entries were transcribed again from the boxes: every one matches what is stored, base for base, including the probe that wraps a line break. The amplicons were re-measured against the same mitochondrial records and come out 98 bp (BdGPL, on CP161941.1) and 64 bp (BdCAPE, on both CP161941.1 and CP161942.1), every primer at zero mismatches. The SEQUENCES_READ_FROM_FIGURE_IMAGE flag stays, because that is still where they come from, but the reading is now corroborated rather than single-source.</p><p>TWO CORRECTIONS TO THE RECORD. First, the paper's own Figure 1 caption says '(a) BdCAPE-specific and (b) BdGPL-specific' while the panels are drawn the other way round - panel (a) carries BdGPL_Fwd, MGB_BdGPL and BdGPL_Rev, panel (b) the BdCAPE set.
