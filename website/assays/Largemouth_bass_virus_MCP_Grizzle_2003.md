## Mismatch distribution

Each oligo drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the scoring
penalizes more heavily, and it depends on the oligo. For a primer it is the 3′ window,
where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site. For a probe it is the central
third: a hydrolysis probe is never extended, its 3′ end carries the quencher and it is
cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex stability. Bar height is the
share of records that differ at that position. Bar color is what that costs, measured
as the fall in detection between records that differ there and records that do not:

<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-strong"></span> detection falls by 25 points or more</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-mid"></span> falls 10 to 25</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-weak"></span> little or no effect: variation the assay tolerates</span>

Hover for counts. A tall gray bar is a polymorphism the assay tolerates; a short red
bar is a position that matters.

## What was deposited, by lineage

Lineages here are sequence groups, not species or taxids. Each is a group of records
that look alike over the amplicon and its flanks with the oligo sites masked, cut at
0.05 Mash distance. Records can link one species to the next, so a group
can hold several; a percent in its name is the share of its top species. The groups
come from sequence this assay does not measure, so the mix below can move without the
assay changing. The dashed line on the chart above recomputes each year on the whole
period's mix: where the two lines part, what got sequenced changed, not what the assay
detects.

<details class="aw-curation"><summary>The full note, as the curator wrote it · in the order the curator wrote it, oldest first</summary><p><b>2026-10-04</b> Added 2026-10-04 from Grizzle et al. 2003, read from the paper's own Methods, which prints both sequences in the running text. This is the canonical LMBV-specific conventional PCR used by US fish-health laboratories. Product 248 bp, stated in the paper and independently measured here. VERIFIED against the paper's own design sequence: both primers match AF080250 - the accession Grizzle names as the design target - with ZERO mismatches, giving exactly 248 bp. That is the cleanest confirmation of a transcription this registry has, since the paper tells you which record it designed against and the primers land on it perfectly.</p><p>SCOPE IS DELIBERATELY NARROWER THAN THE SPECIES, because the paper's claim is. Grizzle chose these primers to match 'a portion of the LMBV genome that was distinct from the sequence of doctorfish virus and guppy virus', and Fig. 1 shows they do not amplify either, nor frog virus 3, nor EHNV, nor red seabream iridovirus - even though Mao et al. 1999 had concluded doctorfish and guppy viruses are the same species as LMBV. So non-detection of doctorfish or guppy virus is this assay working as designed, and they are excluded from the denominator: the query covers the bass and Santee-Cooper nodes only (114 records) and leaves out doctorfish, guppy and guppyfish iridovirus (9 records). Screening confirms the exclusion is real rather than nominal: doctorfish and guppy virus each carry 6 mismatches across the pair. Contrast Ranavirus_micropterus1_MCP_Li_2025, which detects both and is therefore a group-level assay, not an LMBV-specific one - the two entries answer different questions and should not be compared head to head without that in mind.</p><p>POSSIBLE REAL DRIFT, and the reason this entry is worth watching. Against the 1990s US sequence it was designed on, the fit is perfect. Against contemporary LMBV genomes it is not: PX879566.1 (2025) carries 4 forward and 1 reverse mismatches, and PV833272.1 (2024) has no 248 bp site at all within the search budget. That is the same order of divergence as the doctorfish and guppy viruses this assay was built to exclude, which would mean its discrimination has eroded from both sides. Treat as a hypothesis until the pipeline has run this group properly - the numbers above come from four hand-checked genomes, not from a screened archive. Note Grizzle also ran a beta-actin internal control (632 bp, L36342) alongside; host-gene controls are out of scope for this registry and are not entered.</p></details>
