## The verdict, and the numbers behind it

**declining** — below the threshold, measurably lower than a baseline that once cleared it

Everything the call was made from, so you can check it yourself:

Change from baseline, Newcombe interval: **-7.2% to -5.0%**. Threshold: **90.0%**.

*pooled share 30.0% has low coverage, but lineage Orthoflavivirus japonicum (3048158) (186 records) detects only 4.3% (2.2%-8.3%), down from 59.7% on 494 earlier records; lineage verdict overrides to declining*

Trend across deposit years: by record falling (p=5.04e-97, 40 years); by footprint falling (p=0.000662). Shown for context; it does not feed the verdict.

## What reached the statistics

61,192 records were screened for this assay and **34,116 (56%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 27,076 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one drifted primer counts as a failure while a record with both drifted leaves the denominator entirely. The bias is known, measured and not obviously fixable — see [known limitations](../what-the-output-is-worth.md).
