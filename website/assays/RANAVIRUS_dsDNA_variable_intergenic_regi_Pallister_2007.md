## Mismatch distribution

Each oligonucleotide drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the
scoring penalizes more heavily, and it differs by oligonucleotide: for a primer the
3′ window, where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site; for a probe the
central third, since a hydrolysis probe is never extended — its 3′ end carries the
quencher and it is cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex
stability. Bar height is the proportion of records differing at that position; bar
color is the consequence, measured as the fall in detection between records
differing there and those not:

<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-strong"></span> detection falls by 25 points or more</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-mid"></span> falls 10 to 25</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-weak"></span> little or no effect — variation the assay tolerates</span>

Counts are available on hover. A tall gray bar indicates a tolerated polymorphism; a
short red bar indicates a position of consequence.

## By lineage

Every evaluable record is one lineage, **Ranavirus micropterus1 (3432136)** (55 of 59 detected, 93.2%, 95% interval 83.8–97.3%), so splitting by lineage adds nothing to the figure above. The split matters where an assay spans several clades: a pooled percentage can look healthy while one lineage fails completely.


## Scope

This entry declares no scope restriction, so every record retrieved for its target taxid counts toward the denominator. Where an assay was designed for a narrower range than its taxid covers, that presents as low coverage rather than drift; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).

Disclosures on this entry: `amplicon_multimodal`, `oligos_from_secondary_source`.


## Records reaching the statistics

1,467 records were screened for this assay and **250 (17%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 1,217 produced no amplicon at all.

This is material because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

PROMOTION TO ACTIVE ATTEMPTED AND WITHDRAWN 2026-10-21. 

<details class="aw-curation"><summary>The full note, as the curator wrote it · in the order the curator wrote it, oldest first</summary><p><b>2026-10-21</b> PROMOTION TO ACTIVE ATTEMPTED AND WITHDRAWN 2026-10-21. The drift evidence supports it - detected share 90.0% (77%-96%) on 40 evaluable records in the 24-month window, status stable - and the entry meets every other schema precondition (taxid 10492, DOI verified, design_year 2007). It is held at draft by the amplicon defect below, found while checking the entry for promotion, which the validator correctly refuses to let an `active` entry carry. AMPLICON: THE DECLARED SCALAR IS WRONG AND IS HIDING RECORDS. Pallister et al. 2007 answers this directly (papers/, p. 4): "The amplification product obtained using the 16R and 16F primers was substantially smaller for BIV than for EHNV, ECV and ESV - around 200 bp for BIV, approximately 700 bp for EHNV and slightly larger for ECV and ESV." So the product length is SPECIES-DEPENDENT by design - this is the variable intergenic region, and the variation is the locus - and 200 bp is the BIV figure only. Measured on the 2026-10-21 run: of 128 records yielding a product the sizes are 244 bp (44), 228 (39), 212 (37), 227 (6), 209 (1) and 196 (1), all BIV-scale, and separately 61 records carry BOTH primer sites with no facing pair inside the 150-250 bp window the scalar implies ("sites found but no facing pair within 150-250 bp; both primers match"). Those 61 are the EHNV/ECV/ESV end of the assay and are counted as no_amplicon purely because of the declared size - a 48% understatement of the evaluable denominator. RESOLVED 2026-10-21 as amplicon_bp_range [186, 749], RANGE-ONLY. The 10492 archives were rescreened with the size window opened: detect/reduced products jump from 128 to 324 records spanning 196-739 bp - the EHNV/ECV/ESV ~700 bp end Pallister describes, recovered. NO species_sizes, because length does not cleanly name a species here: within Ranavirus micropterus1 alone the product ladders 212-308 bp, so the intergenic length varies by isolate, not by species as Pallister's four reference isotypes suggested. The range is the measured envelope + a 10 bp cushion (config.yaml amplicon: the measured size wins).</p><p><b>2026-10-21</b> PROMOTION STILL WAITS ON THE FULL RESCREEN, now for a different reason: the old 90.0%% stable counted only the BIV-scale products that detect well; including the ~700 bp end, much of which scores reduced or fail, will LOWER the real detected share, so the status has to be re-read on the next run before this is promoted.
