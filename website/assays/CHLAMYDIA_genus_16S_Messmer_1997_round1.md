Calls among product-bearing records: psittaci 108 reduced and 4 fail; pneumoniae 12 reduced and 1 fail; trachomatis 72 fail; other genus members sampled (C. abortus, felis, pecorum, muridarum, avium, gallinacea, uncultured Chlamydia): 20 reduced, 26 fail.
