## Scope — what this assay is judged against

This entry declares no `scope_taxids`, so every record retrieved for its target taxid counts toward the denominator. Where an assay was designed for a narrower set than its taxid covers, that reads as low coverage rather than as drift — see [scoping](../scoping.md).

Retrieval query:

```
(txid768738[Organism:exp] OR txid3050275[Organism:exp]) AND ("DNA polymerase"[Title] OR U38[Gene] OR POL[Gene] OR "complete genome"[Title] OR "partial genome"[Title])
```

A retrieval query is a scientific claim about the population the assay is being judged against, and it can go stale silently — see [scoping](../scoping.md).


## What reached the statistics

20 records were screened for this assay and **10 (50%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 10 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one drifted primer counts as a failure while a record with both drifted leaves the denominator entirely. The bias is known, measured and not obviously fixable — see [known limitations](../limitations.md).
