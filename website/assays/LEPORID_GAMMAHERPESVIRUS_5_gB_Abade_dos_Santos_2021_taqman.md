## Mismatch distribution

Each oligonucleotide drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the
scoring penalizes more heavily, and it differs by oligonucleotide: for a primer the
3′ window, where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site; for a probe the
central third, since a hydrolysis probe is never extended — its 3′ end carries the
quencher and it is cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex
stability. Bar height is the proportion of records differing at that position; bar
color is the consequence, measured as the fall in detection between records
differing there and those not:

## Records reaching the statistics

5 records were screened for this assay and **5 (100%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 0 produced no amplicon at all.

This is material because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).


## Curation record

Added 2026-10-06 from Abade dos Santos et al. 2021 (papers/10.3390_v13040715.pdf), Table 1, a text table (open access). The paper runs one primer pair in two real-time formats: a TaqMan duplex with an 18S internal control (the entry ending _taqman) and an EvaGreen singleplex (the entry ending _evagreen, same primers, no probe). Both entries are kept because they are different assays at the bench (the probe adds a third oligo that can fail on its own); the primer pair itself is identical, so a maintainer who wants one row per pair can drop the _evagreen entry. EXCLUDED: the 18S rRNA Fw/Rv/Probe set (probe Cy5/BHQ2). It is a host (hare/rabbit and other mammals) extraction and amplification control, not a pathogen target; screening it against LeHV-5 records would only report meaningless non-detection. Also not entered: the nested LeHV-5 PCR the paper used as a comparator (its ref. 11, a different paper, whose primers are not printed here).
