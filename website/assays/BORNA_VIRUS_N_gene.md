## Scope — what this assay is judged against

A record counts toward the denominator when its taxid, or any ancestor, is in that set. Records outside it are screened and reported but kept out of the statistics.

1,005 screened records fell outside the declared scope.

Disclosures on this entry: `oligos_not_in_cited_paper`, `orientation_inferred`.


## What reached the statistics

500 records were screened for this assay and **315 (63%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 185 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one drifted primer counts as a failure while a record with both drifted leaves the denominator entirely. The bias is known, measured and not obviously fixable — see [known limitations](../what-the-output-is-worth.md).
