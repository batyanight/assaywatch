## By lineage

Every evaluable record is one lineage, **Morbillivirus canis (3052342)** (75 of 93 detected, 80.6%, 95% interval 71.5–87.4%), so splitting by lineage adds nothing to the figure above. The split matters where an assay spans several clades: a pooled percentage can look healthy while one lineage fails completely.


## Scope — what this assay is judged against

This entry declares **`scope_taxids`**: [3052342](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=3052342). A record counts toward the denominator when its taxid, or any ancestor, is in that set. Records outside it are screened and reported but kept out of the statistics.


## What reached the statistics

3,952 records were screened for this assay and **602 (15%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 3,350 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one drifted primer counts as a failure while a record with both drifted leaves the denominator entirely. The bias is known, measured and not obviously fixable — see [known limitations](../limitations.md).
