## Scope

**Meant to detect:** Filoviruses (Marburg and Ebola viruses), L gene, as a single degenerate pair derived from the published consensus cocktail.  
**Scored against:** every record retrieved.  
**Retrieved from:** Filoviridae (taxid 11266), every record.

This entry declares no scope restriction, so every record retrieved for its target taxid counts toward the denominator. Where an assay was designed for a narrower range than its taxid covers, that presents as low coverage rather than drift; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).

Disclosures on this entry: `oligos_modified`, `amplicon_disagrees_with_paper`, `scope_wider_than_paper_tested`.

## Records reaching the statistics

4,647 records were screened for this assay and **3,577 (77%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 1,070 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

## What was deposited, by lineage

Lineages here are sequence groups, not species or taxids. Each is a group of records
that look alike over the amplicon and its flanks with the oligo sites masked, cut at
0.05 Mash distance. Records can link one species to the next, so a group
can hold several; a percent in its name is the share of its top species. The groups
come from sequence this assay does not measure, so the mix below can move without the
assay changing. The dashed line on the chart above recomputes each year on the whole
period's mix: where the two lines part, what got sequenced changed, not what the assay
detects.

## Curation record

BOTH OLIGOS CONFIRMED 2026-10-23 as a consolidation, not a discrepancy. Zhai et al. 2007 Table 1 prints a 4+4 cocktail of fully-specified primers, not one degenerate pair, so neither stored sequence occurs in it and both read `absent`. Checked base by base, MOD-FWD covers all four printed forwards over its 24 nt with no mismatch and MOD-RVS all four reverses over 23 nt, so the stored pair is the IUPAC consolidation of that cocktail. It is published in that form, named Filo-MOD-FWD and Filo-MOD-RVS and credited to Zhai 2007, in the methods of 10.1038/s41467-020-14327-8 (PMC6981187), 10.1007/s10393-020-01503-x (PMC7682123) and 10.3390/v14030560 (PMC8951055). Those same protocols pair MOD-FWD with FiloL.conR in a first round, so this entry is the inner step of a nested assay and is stored as the round-2 pair only. CORRECTED 2026-11-10: 'validated with all known filovirus strains' (p3) describes 2006; genera named since are untested.
