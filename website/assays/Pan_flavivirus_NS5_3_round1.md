## The verdict, and the numbers behind it

**declining** — below the threshold, measurably lower than a baseline that once cleared it

Everything the call was made from, so you can check it yourself:

| | detected | evaluable | share | 95% interval |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| recent window (24 months, from Oct 2024) | 5,682 | 9,503 | 59.8% | 58.8% – 60.8% |
| baseline (everything earlier) | 12,995 | 24,647 | 52.7% | 52.1% – 53.3% |

Change from baseline, Newcombe interval: **5.9% to 8.2%**. Threshold: **90.0%**.

*pooled share 59.8% has low coverage, but lineage Orthoflavivirus usutuense (3048443) (428 records) detects only 0.0% (0.0%-0.9%), down from 12.3% on 592 earlier records; lineage verdict overrides to declining*

Trend across deposit years: by record rising (p=1.28e-06, 40 years); by footprint falling (p=0.000632); **the two disagree on direction**. Shown for context; it does not feed the verdict.

## What reached the statistics

61,192 records were screened for this assay and **34,150 (56%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 27,042 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one drifted primer counts as a failure while a record with both drifted leaves the denominator entirely. The bias is known, measured and not obviously fixable — see [known limitations](../what-the-output-is-worth.md).
