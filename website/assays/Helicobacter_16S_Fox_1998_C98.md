This matters because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

## What was deposited, by lineage

Lineages here are sequence groups, not species or taxids. Each is a group of records
that look alike over the amplicon and its flanks with the oligo sites masked, cut at
0.05 Mash distance. Records can link one species to the next, so a group
can hold several; a percent in its name is the share of its top species. The groups
come from sequence this assay does not measure, so the mix below can move without the
assay changing. The dashed line on the chart above recomputes each year on the whole
period's mix: where the two lines part, what got sequenced changed, not what the assay
detects.

**The largest group here, H. pylori 26%, holds several species names,** which may be
different species or one species under more than one name. The dashed line can't separate
a shift between species within a group. The table below shows the names in each group.

<details class="aw-curation"><summary>The full note, as the curator wrote it · in the order the curator wrote it, oldest first</summary><p><b>2026-10-19</b> Added 2026-10-19 from candidate oligo extraction (papers/10.1016_s0016-5085_98_70589-x.pdf). The paper states the C97+C98 pair produces "approximately 400" bases and the C97+C05 pair "approximately 1200" bases. 16S rRNA positions from paper: C97 at 276-291 (forward), C98 at 681-698 (reverse). There is no results/screened/209/all directory at all, which is the proof.</p><p>THE FIX IS THE SECOND OPTION THE WARNING OFFERS, not the first. target.query is now character-for-character the query its sibling Helicobacter_16S_Fox_1998_C05 already carries, which Helicobacter_16S_Conley_2021 carries too, so the 16S query is the established pattern for this taxon in this registry. Adding 209 to large_taxa_allowed was rejected: it would fetch 843,447 records to screen a 16S assay whose product is 398 bp, and the sibling already demonstrates the query is sufficient.
