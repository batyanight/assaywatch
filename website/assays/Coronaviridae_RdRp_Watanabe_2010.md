**Coronaviridae — RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (Watanabe 2010)**

Conventional one-step-then-PCR (35 cycles, 50 C annealing) consensus primers against a conserved region of the RNA-dependent RNA polymerase gene of coronaviruses. They were used in earlier surveys and applied here to screen bat intestine for coronavirus RNA.

Each oligo drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the scoring
penalizes more heavily, and it depends on the oligo. For a primer it is the 3′ window,
where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site. For a probe it is the central
third: a hydrolysis probe is never extended, its 3′ end carries the quencher and it is
cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex stability. Bar height is the
share of records that differ at that position. Bar color is what that costs, measured
as the fall in detection between records that differ there and records that do not:

<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-strong"></span> detection falls by 25 points or more</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-mid"></span> falls 10 to 25</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-weak"></span> little or no effect: variation the assay tolerates</span>

Hover for counts. A tall gray bar is a polymorphism the assay tolerates; a short red
bar is a position that matters.


## Verdict

**declining** — below the threshold, and measurably lower than a baseline that previously exceeded it

Every value behind the call, so you can redo it by hand:

Change from baseline, Newcombe interval: **-9.1% to -3.9%**. Threshold: **90.0%**.

*pooled share 41.4% has low coverage, but lineage Bat coronavirus (1508220) (172 records) detects only 25.6% (19.6%-32.6%), down from 75.4% on 175 earlier records; lineage verdict overrides to declining*

Trend across deposit years: by record rising (p = 0.0595, 30 years); by footprint falling (p = 0.812); **the two disagree on direction**. Reported for context; it is not an input to the verdict.

From the 2026-11-10 run. A status tells you where to look; it is not a result. [Known limitations](../limitations.md) reports how often these calls are correct.


## Scope

**Meant to detect:** Coronaviruses, RdRp gene, as a consensus screen.  
**Scored against:** every record retrieved.  
**Retrieved from:** Coronaviridae (taxid 11118), limited by a query on the title or gene.

This entry declares no scope restriction, so every record retrieved for its target taxid counts toward the denominator. Where an assay was designed for a narrower range than its taxid covers, that presents as low coverage rather than drift; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).

```
(RdRp[Gene] OR orf1ab[Gene] OR ORF1ab[Title] OR "RNA-dependent RNA polymerase"[Title] OR "complete genome"[Title]) NOT txid2697049[Organism:exp]
```

A retrieval query is a claim about which records the assay is tested against, and it can stop being accurate without producing an error; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).

## Records reaching the statistics

25,512 records were screened for this assay and **7,942 (31%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 17,570 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

## What was deposited, by lineage

Lineages here are sequence groups, not species or taxids. Each is a group of records
that look alike over the amplicon and its flanks with the oligo sites masked, cut at
0.05 Mash distance. Records can link one species to the next, so a group
can hold several; a percent in its name is the share of its top species. The groups
come from sequence this assay does not measure, so the mix below can move without the
assay changing. The dashed line on the chart above recomputes each year on the whole
period's mix: where the two lines part, what got sequenced changed, not what the assay
detects.
