## The verdict, and the numbers behind it

**insufficient data** — too few evaluable records to say anything; reported, never flagged

Everything the call was made from, so you can check it yourself:

| | detected | evaluable | share | 95% interval |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| recent window (60 months, from Oct 2021) | 3 | 14 | 21.4% | 7.6% – 47.6% |
| baseline (everything earlier) | 5 | 76 | 6.6% | 2.8% – 14.5% |

Change from baseline, Newcombe interval: **-1.1% to 41.3%**. Threshold: **90.0%**.

*14 evaluable records since Oct 2021, below the minimum of 15; the detected share is 21% (8%-48%), too wide to act on (window widened to 60 months to reach 15 evaluable records)*

Trend across deposit years: by record falling (p=0.414, 22 years); by footprint rising (p=0.83); **the two disagree on direction**. Shown for context; it does not feed the verdict.

From the 2026-10-31 run. A status tells you where to look rather than what is true — [what the output is worth](../what-the-output-is-worth.md) explains how often these calls are right.
