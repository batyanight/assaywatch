## Records reaching the statistics

2,083 records were screened for this assay and **90 (4%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 1,993 produced no amplicon at all.

Non-authentic (cloning-tail) bases are marked in the table by a distinct italic font, and NONE of the primers in this entry carries one except where tail_5prime is set below. amplicon_bp is COMPUTED, not quoted: the paper gives 3' positions, not product sizes, so the product is taken as the span from the forward primer's 5' base to the reverse primer's 5' base using the printed 3' positions and the primer lengths. BUN-S5-F carries a 5 nt italic tail (GCCGC); its 20 nt of authentic sequence has its 3' base at 20, so it binds 1-20, the M-segment 5' terminus. M940C-R is 26 nt with its 3' base at 936, so it binds 936-961; the product is 1-961 = 961 bp. The arithmetic is corroborated by the primer names, which encode their own positions (BUNS-3-L947 at 947, BUNL650R at 601-630). Treat the size as approximate: it is exact only on the Bunyamwera reference, and these are consensus primers meant for a whole serogroup. SCOPE. The paper designed these from an alignment of 'orthobunyaviruses of the CE and BUN serogroups and Guaroa virus', so the claim is the California encephalitis and Bunyamwera serogroups, not the whole genus. The registry target is genus Orthobunyavirus (~18 serogroups), so a low detected share here is very likely scope rather than drift. Screening the two assays already in the registry on 2026-10-04 showed the pattern plainly: strong on the design serogroups (Cache Valley 14/15, Maguari 4/5) and near-zero on Simbu-serogroup viruses (Oropouche 0/24, Akabane 0/7, Schmallenberg 0/7, Manzanilla 0/4). Left unscoped pending the registry-wide decision on lineage scoping. Anchored on the conserved M-segment terminus, like the S-segment assay already in the registry, so most partial-CDS deposits will return no_amplicon however well the primers match. Screened on 2026-10-04: 93 evaluable of 2,056, 8 detected. The low evaluable count is the terminal anchoring, as expected.</p></details>
