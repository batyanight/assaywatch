## Curation record

Primers 23SF and 23SR were designed by Zhu et al. 

<details class="aw-curation"><summary>The full note, as the curator wrote it · in the order the curator wrote it, oldest first</summary><p><b>2026-10-23</b> Primers 23SF and 23SR were designed by Zhu et al. 2000 (Microbiology 146:315-321). Schrenzel et al. 2005 (10.1645/ge-3310) used them and cited "as described (Zhu et al., 2000)". The seed list cited Schrenzel 2005. 23SR CONFIRMED 2026-10-23. It read `absent` only because Zhu et al. 2000 writes its degenerate positions as parenthesised alternatives - TTT(C/T)(C/T)G(A/G)TCCTCTCGTACT in Table 1, PCR no. 4 (LSU rRNA, antisense) - which expands to exactly the stored TTTYYGRTCCTCTCGTACT. No oligo-like run of that shape survives the extraction, so the verifier had nothing to match. Nothing is in doubt, and the entry's existing account of Zhu 2000 as the design paper is right.</p></details>
