Each oligo drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the scoring
penalizes more heavily, and it depends on the oligo. For a primer it is the 3′ window,
where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site. For a probe it is the central
third: a hydrolysis probe is never extended, its 3′ end carries the quencher and it is
cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex stability. Bar height is the
share of records that differ at that position. Bar color is what that costs, measured
as the fall in detection between records that differ there and records that do not:

## Records reaching the statistics

2,974 records were screened for this assay and **1,102 (37%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 1,872 produced no amplicon at all.

## What was deposited, by lineage

Lineages here are sequence groups, not species or taxids. Each is a group of records
that look alike over the amplicon and its flanks with the oligo sites masked, cut at
0.05 Mash distance. Records can link one species to the next, so a group
can hold several; a percent in its name is the share of its top species. The groups
come from sequence this assay does not measure, so the mix below can move without the
assay changing. The dashed line on the chart above recomputes each year on the whole
period's mix: where the two lines part, what got sequenced changed, not what the assay
detects.

different species or one species under more than one name. The dashed line can't separate
a shift between species within a group. The table below shows the names in each group.

This representative sequence was deposited in GenBank under accession number MG910456." That record is titled "Uncultured Helicobacteraceae bacterium clone GCBI1 (2595) 16S ribosomal RNA gene", 1,422 bp, and GCBI1 is the paper's own provisional name for the organism. MEASURED on MG910456.1: all three oligos are exact and correctly oriented, Helico-New-F at 99..130, the probe at 150..173 and Helico-New-R as its reverse complement at 267..289, with the probe inside the product. So the assay works on its own target and the 0.0 percent this entry reports is DEFINITIVELY scope, not drift, and not an oligo problem.</p><p>WHY THE GENUS ARCHIVE CANNOT SEE IT: MG910456 carries taxid 290433, "uncultured Helicobacteraceae bacterium", whose lineage is Campylobacterales &gt; Helicobacteraceae &gt; environmental samples. It is NOT under genus Helicobacter 209, so this entry's retrieval term txid209[Organism:exp] provably cannot reach it. That is now flagged as retrieval_scope_incomplete.</p><p>AMPLICON CORRECTED 194 -&gt; 191, and this is the measurement winning over the publication exactly as config.yaml's amplicon section and CLAUDE.md require. The paper states the primers "amplified a 194bp region"; on the paper's own reference sequence the product is 191 bp. amplicon_disagrees_with_paper is set and both numbers are recorded here. This also revises the earlier guess above that "194 minus the 14 bp insertion is 180, which is exactly the second peak": the true product on the target is 191, so the insertion-free length is 177, and check_amplicons.py's two genus-wide peaks at 178 and 180 bp are non-target Helicobacter rather than an exact arithmetic match to anything.</p><p><b>2026-10-24</b> NO SCOPE FIX IS POSSIBLE HERE, and docs/journal/2026-10-24-scope-audit.md item 4 was WRONG to propose taxid 290433. 290433 is not a GCBI1 node, it is a generic catch-all for uncultured Helicobacteraceae, and its records are unrelated environmental and gut survey clones - CT_027, VB_018, MS-A162, SRNO176B04c, Flu2_38, F2M5Y2A01AY14G and so on. Retargeting this entry at 290433 would replace a genus denominator with a grab-bag denominator: still not the target organism, and less interpretable, not more. There is no NCBI taxon for Helicobacter sp. GCBI1, so no scope_taxids list can express this assay's scope, and scope_taxids is deliberately NOT set. The honest position is the one now recorded in the flags: the assay is verified against its reference, its target has one record which its own retrieval cannot reach, and its detected share against genus Helicobacter is not a coverage figure and should not be read as one. What would actually fix it is a maintainer decision outside this entry: either an accession-level scope mechanism, which the schema does not have, or broadening target.taxid to Helicobacteraceae so the reference is at least screened - which makes the reference reachable but the denominator worse, and so is not done unilaterally.</p><p>ONE MORE DISCREPANCY, recorded not fixed: the stored oligo names are Helico-New-F, Helico-New-R and PROBE1, while the paper prints GCBI-Helico-F and GCBI-Helico-R and leaves the probe unnamed. The SEQUENCES all verify exact against the paper, so this is a naming difference only; the note above saying the paper "prints all three oligos exactly as stored" is true of the sequences and not of the names. Renaming is cheap but oligo names can be referenced by test fixtures, so it is left for a maintainer. The chemistry IS right, unlike the TINC entry: the paper prints 5'-6FAM-...-MGBNFQ-3', so mgb and MGB-NFQ are correct.</p></details>
