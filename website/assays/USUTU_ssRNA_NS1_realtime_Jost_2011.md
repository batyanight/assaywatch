## By lineage

Every evaluable record is one lineage, **Orthoflavivirus usutuense (3048443)** (353 of 388 detected, 91.0%, 95% interval 87.7–93.4%), so splitting by lineage adds nothing to the figure above. The split matters where an assay spans several clades: a pooled percentage can look healthy while one lineage fails completely.
