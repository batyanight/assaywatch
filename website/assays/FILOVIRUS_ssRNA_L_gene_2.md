## Mismatch distribution

Each oligonucleotide drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the
scoring penalizes more heavily, and it differs by oligonucleotide: for a primer the
3′ window, where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site; for a probe the
central third, since a hydrolysis probe is never extended — its 3′ end carries the
quencher and it is cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex
stability. Bar height is the proportion of records differing at that position; bar
color is the consequence, measured as the fall in detection between records
differing there and those not:

short red bar indicates a position of consequence.

## Records reaching the statistics

4,647 records were screened for this assay and **3,577 (77%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 1,070 produced no amplicon at all.

This is material because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

## Curation record

BOTH OLIGOS CONFIRMED 2026-10-23 as a consolidation, not a discrepancy. Zhai et al. 2007 Table 1 prints a 4+4 cocktail of fully-specified primers, not one degenerate pair, so neither stored sequence occurs in it and both read `absent`. Checked base by base, MOD-FWD covers all four printed forwards over its 24 nt with no mismatch and MOD-RVS all four reverses over 23 nt, so the stored pair is the IUPAC consolidation of that cocktail. It is published in that form, named Filo-MOD-FWD and Filo-MOD-RVS and credited to Zhai 2007, in the methods of 10.1038/s41467-020-14327-8 (PMC6981187), 10.1007/s10393-020-01503-x (PMC7682123) and 10.3390/v14030560 (PMC8951055). Those same protocols pair MOD-FWD with FiloL.conR in a first round, so this entry is the inner step of a nested assay and is stored as the round-2 pair only.
