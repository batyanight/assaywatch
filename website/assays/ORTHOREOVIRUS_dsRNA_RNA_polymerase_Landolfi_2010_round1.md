## Mismatch distribution

Each oligonucleotide drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the
scoring penalizes more heavily, and it differs by oligonucleotide: for a primer the
3′ window, where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site; for a probe the
central third, since a hydrolysis probe is never extended — its 3′ end carries the
quencher and it is cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex
stability. Bar height is the proportion of records differing at that position; bar
color is the consequence, measured as the fall in detection between records
differing there and those not:

healthy while one clade fails completely. Worst first. Lineages with too few records
to say anything are left out, and a lineage whose whole interval sits below 90% is marked.

## Records reaching the statistics

14,479 records were screened for this assay and **985 (7%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 13,494 produced no amplicon at all.

This is material because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

The methods are explicit and this is a SEMI-NESTED design sharing one forward primer: "The first round utilized forward primer 2090F ... and reverse primer 2334R ..., which was the second round of the original protocol. The second round utilized 2090F and a different reverse primer, 2200R". This entry is the FIRST round, 2090F x 2334R; the base entry holds the second round, 2090F x 2200R, which is the pair it stored all along. THE 2334R REVERSE HAD BEEN SITTING IN THE BASE ENTRY'S oligos.alternates, where score.oligo_pools treated it as an interchangeable member of one pool with 2200R and took whichever product scored better - so a 274 bp round-1 product was being credited to an entry declaring 163 bp. An alternate that makes a different product is a round, not a pool member.</p><p>AMPLICON MEASURED, BECAUSE THE PAPER GIVES NO SIZE FOR ROUND 1. scripts/check_amplicons.py over the Orthoreovirus archive measures 2090F x 2334R at a modal 274 bp (216 records at 3 mismatches). The paper's only stated product is the second round's, and only after processing: the abstract reports a "109-base pair (bp) product", while the round-2 pair measures 161 bp - the difference is the two 26-mer primers, since the methods say "primer sequences were edited out prior to analyses" (161 - 26 - 26 = 109). That arithmetic is the reason to trust the base entry's stored 163 and this entry's measured 274.</p><p>WHY THE ORIGINAL PROTOCOL MATTERS FOR READING THIS. Landolfi adapted a published protocol and deliberately chose a shorter target "to increase chances of success with probable shorter fragments of RNA present in FFPE tissues", promoting that protocol's second round to its own first round. So round 1 here is not the ancestral outer PCR, and a drift verdict on it speaks to this adaptation rather than to the original assay.</p></details>
