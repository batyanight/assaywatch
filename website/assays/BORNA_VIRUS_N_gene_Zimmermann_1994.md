## Mismatch distribution

Each oligonucleotide drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the
scoring penalizes more heavily, and it differs by oligonucleotide: for a primer the
3′ window, where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site; for a probe the
central third, since a hydrolysis probe is never extended — its 3′ end carries the
quencher and it is cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex
stability. Bar height is the proportion of records differing at that position; bar
color is the consequence, measured as the fall in detection between records
differing there and those not:

<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-strong"></span> detection falls by 25 points or more</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-mid"></span> falls 10 to 25</span>
<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-weak"></span> little or no effect — variation the assay tolerates</span>

Counts are available on hover. A tall gray bar indicates a tolerated polymorphism; a
short red bar indicates a position of consequence.

## Records reaching the statistics

500 records were screened for this assay and **312 (62%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 188 produced no amplicon at all.

This is material because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

<details class="aw-curation"><summary>The full note, as the curator wrote it · in the order the curator wrote it, oldest first</summary><p><b>2026-10-24</b> SCOPED 2026-10-24 to scope_taxids [1714619, 12455]. taxid 178830 is the whole Bornaviridae family, while Zimmermann et al. 1994 is a Borna disease virus assay: its title is "Detection of Borna disease virus RNA in naturally infected animals by nested PCR". Both taxids are needed and the second is the trap: 1714619 is Orthobornavirus bornaense (rank species, under Bornaviridae &gt; Orthobornavirus), but 12455 "Borna disease virus" sits under Bornaviridae &gt; UNCLASSIFIED Bornaviridae, not under the genus, so a scope list built from the genus node alone silently drops 49 genuine BoDV-1 deposits. Both taxids verified live with efetch -db taxonomy on 2026-10-24. Measured over the WHOLE 2026-10-24 calls table, which is neither the report's recent window nor patent-filtered: of 1,519 screened records 1,003 are a known taxid outside the scope, 513 are inside it and 3 carry no taxid, so about two thirds of the denominator was a different virus - carbovirus, cultervirus and the parrot, serin and estrildid orthobornaviruses. Note that drift.py's filter drops only a KNOWN out-of-scope taxid, so the 3 unknowns stay in.</p></details>
