The flag is therefore `oligos_modified`, not `oligo_sequence_uncertain`: nothing about the reading is uncertain any more.</p><p>WHAT IS STILL OPEN: whether the published G is a typesetting error in the 1995 paper or the primer its authors really used, which no document on disk can answer -- they report the assay amplifying a 350 bp fragment from every calicivirus they tested, which a 3'-terminally mismatched primer would not do at the 55 C and 60 C annealing temperatures the paper also prints.</p></details>
