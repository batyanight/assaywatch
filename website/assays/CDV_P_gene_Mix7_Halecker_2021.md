## By lineage

Every evaluable record is one lineage, **Morbillivirus canis (3052342)** (85 of 86 detected, 98.8%, 95% interval 93.7–99.8%), so splitting by lineage adds nothing to the figure above. The split matters where an assay spans several clades: a pooled percentage can look healthy while one lineage fails completely.


## Scope

This entry declares no scope restriction, so every record retrieved for its target taxid counts toward the denominator. Where an assay was designed for a narrower range than its taxid covers, that presents as low coverage rather than drift; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).


## Records reaching the statistics

3,952 records were screened for this assay and **373 (9%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 3,579 produced no amplicon at all.

This is material because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).
