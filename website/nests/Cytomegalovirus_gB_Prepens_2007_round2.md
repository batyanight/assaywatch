## Mismatch distribution

Each oligo drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the scoring
penalizes more heavily, and it depends on the oligo. For a primer it is the 3′ window,
where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site. For a probe it is the central
third: a hydrolysis probe is never extended, its 3′ end carries the quencher and it is
cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex stability. Bar height is the
share of records that differ at that position. Bar color is what that costs, measured
as the fall in detection between records that differ there and records that do not:

<span class="aw-key"><span class="aw-key-swatch aw-bar-weak"></span> little or no effect: variation the assay tolerates</span>

Every evaluable record is one lineage, **Cytomegalovirus humanbeta5 (3050295)** (31 of 32 detected, 96.9%, 95% interval 84.3–99.4%), so splitting by lineage adds nothing to the figure above. The split matters where an assay spans several clades: a pooled percentage can look healthy while one lineage fails completely.


## Scope

This protocol's scope is the intersection of its rounds' scopes: a record counts only if every round claims it, so a record one round's target covers and another's does not is out of scope rather than a failure.

The protocol declares no scope restriction, so every record retrieved for its target taxid counts toward the denominator. Where an assay was designed for a narrower range than its taxid covers, that presents as low coverage rather than drift; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).

Disclosures on this entry: `amplicon_measured_not_published`.


## Records reaching the statistics

675 records were screened for this assay and **675 (100%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 0 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).
