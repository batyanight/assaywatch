## Scope

This protocol's scope is the intersection of its rounds' scopes: a record counts only if every round claims it, so a record one round's target covers and another's does not is out of scope rather than a failure.

**Meant to detect:** Rhabdoviruses, L gene, as a consensus screen.  
**Scored against:** only taxa that every round claims.  
**Retrieved from:** Rhabdoviridae (taxid 11270), limited by a query on the title or gene.

The protocol declares **`scope_taxids`**: <a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=35303" title="unclassified Rhabdoviridae">35303</a>, <a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=2842407">2842407</a>, <a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=2842415">2842415</a>, <a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=3152207">3152207</a>. A record counts toward the denominator when its taxid, or any ancestor, is in that set. Records outside it are screened and reported but kept out of the statistics.

## Records reaching the statistics

6,501 records were screened for this assay and **6,501 (100%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 0 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

## What was deposited, by lineage

Lineages here are sequence groups, not species or taxids. Each is a group of records
that look alike over the amplicon and its flanks with the oligo sites masked, cut at
0.05 Mash distance. Records can link one species to the next, so a group
can hold several; a percent in its name is the share of its top species. The groups
come from sequence this assay does not measure, so the mix below can move without the
assay changing. The dashed line on the chart above recomputes each year on the whole
period's mix: where the two lines part, what got sequenced changed, not what the assay
detects.

*244 of 6634 records unplaced (4%); 47 of 6634 diverged, with no site to cluster on.*
