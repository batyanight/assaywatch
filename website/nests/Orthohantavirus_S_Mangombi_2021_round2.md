## Mismatch distribution

Each oligo drawn base by base, 5′ to 3′. The shaded region is the one the scoring
penalizes more heavily, and it depends on the oligo. For a primer it is the 3′ window,
where a mismatch sits in the polymerase’s catalytic site. For a probe it is the central
third: a hydrolysis probe is never extended, its 3′ end carries the quencher and it is
cleaved by exonuclease activity, so what matters is duplex stability. Bar height is the
share of records that differ at that position. Bar color is what that costs, measured
as the fall in detection between records that differ there and records that do not:

## Scope

This protocol's scope is the intersection of its rounds' scopes: a record counts only if every round claims it, so a record one round's target covers and another's does not is out of scope rather than a failure.

The protocol declares no scope restriction, so every record retrieved for its target taxid counts toward the denominator. Where an assay was designed for a narrower range than its taxid covers, that presents as low coverage rather than drift; see [scope and retrieval](../scoping.md).

Disclosures on this entry: `nested_protocol`.


## Records reaching the statistics

4,143 records were screened for this assay and **4,143 (100%)** were evaluable, meaning both primers landed in the right orientation and size range. 0 produced no amplicon at all.

This matters because a record with one diverged primer is scored as a failure while a record with both diverged is excluded from the denominator entirely. The resulting bias is known, measured, and may not be correctable; see [known limitations](../limitations.md).

That table credits them to Aitichou et al. 2005 (J Virol Methods), doi 10.1016/j.jviromet.2004.10.004, and the paper was read on 2026-11-01: THE CREDIT IS HALF RIGHT. Aitichou's Table 1 prints HANTAV1U as GWGGVCARACAGCWGAYT (S segment 374-391) and HANTAV1L as TCCWGGTGTAADYTCHTCWGC (604-624). The stored FORWARD is HANTAV1U: aligned from its third base it is compatible at all 16 overlapping positions, with two widened (A to W, W to H) and two further bases GG on the 3' end, so it is a modified, shifted HANTAV1U. The stored REVERSE is NOT Aitichou's: it shares only an 8 nt overlap (TC.TC.GC) with the 3' end of HANTAV1L and runs downstream from there, and against every primer and probe Aitichou prints its nearest match is 7 incompatible positions. So Aitichou is cited here as the forward's origin and not as this pair's source, and Mangombi 2021 is cited because it is the only publication that prints the pair. Aitichou is a real-time TaqMan assay for four named hantaviruses; this is a conventional universal pair, which is the second reason the attribution cannot be taken whole. The reverse reads near:1 against the paper and that is notation, not a difference: Mangombi prints UHantaR2 with inosine (TCITCWGCYTTCATDSTDGA), kept in seq_as_written, while seq stores the N form this registry screens with. Amplicon confirmed by measurement 2026-11-01: the stored 236 bp is the modal product in 4,122 of 4,128.
