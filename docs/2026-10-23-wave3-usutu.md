# Wave 3: the Usutu false alarm is a scoring false-fail (2026-10-23)

`Pan_flavivirus_NS5_3_round1` and `_round2` are `declining` as of 2017-03-31, and both
are `works` cases in `docs/validation-cases.tsv`: in the MediLabSecure external quality
assessment (Perez-Ramirez 2020, doi:10.3390/pathogens9121038) the reference lab running
this hemi-nested NS5 PCR detected all eight positive panel samples, Usutu among them.
The flag comes from the lineage check: *Orthoflavivirus usutuense* detects 0.0% (0.0%-0.9%)
of 428 recent records, down from 12.0% earlier. Wave 1 established the interval was not
the problem. This establishes what is.

## The screen fails Usutu on one recurring footprint

Of the 1,974 records whose taxid rolls up to *O. usutuense* (taxid 3048443),
`Pan_flavivirus_NS5_3_round1` (Scaramozzino 2001, MAMD / cFD2, conventional PCR, 263 bp)
calls 951 `fail` and 71 `detect` or `reduced`. The `fail`s carry nearly one footprint:

| oligo | mismatches, distance from 3' | mispair (primer:template) | records |
|---|---|---|---:|
| forward MAMD, 23-mer, degenerate | 8, 6 | A:G, R:A | 947 of 951 |
| reverse cFD2, 26-mer | 12, **3** | G:A, **A:A** | 878 of 951; most of the rest add one further mismatch (d = 9, 15, 18 or 24) |

None is terminal. Under the budget engine: forward 1.0 + 1.0 = 2.0; reverse 3.0 (d = 3,
inside the 5-nt 3' window) + 0.5 = 3.5; total 5.5 against `reduced_max: 3.0`, so
`fail`.

## The thermodynamics say both primers still bind

primer3 nearest-neighbour, `config.yaml` `thermo_conditions`, on 40 sampled `fail`
records:

| oligo | Tm on a perfect site | dTm on Usutu | Tm on Usutu |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| forward MAMD | 58.6 C | -8 C (40/40) | ~50.6 C |
| reverse cFD2 | 76.7 C | -8 C (39/40) | ~68.7 C |

The reverse duplex keeps roughly 69 C. The footprint is near-universal across deposited
Usutu, so the EQA strain almost certainly carried it, and the bench amplified it. **The
scorer calls `fail` on a template the bench demonstrably amplifies.**

## Which rule does it

- **The 3' window is flat.** `primer_3prime_penalty: 3.0` applies equally at d = 1 and
  d = 5, so the A:A at d = 3 alone costs exactly `reduced_max`. Kwok 1990 and
  Stadhouders 2010 measure the severe classes (A:A, A:G, G:A, C:C) as severe **at the
  terminus**; the effect decays over the last few bases. A step function cannot express
  that decay; this is the case it misprices.
- **The budget sums across primers** (README guess 2). The `serial` engine does not
  rescue it, though: the reverse primer alone scores 3.5, above `oligo_weak_max: 3.0`.
- **One table for conventional and probe chemistry** (README guess 3). The
  conventional override tried and reverted on 2026-10-19 (`config.yaml:240`:
  `primer_3prime_penalty: 2.0`, `reduced_max: 5.0`) scores this 2.5 + 2.0 = 4.5, i.e.
  `reduced`. It was reverted because, on the 49 cases, it traded catches for false
  alarms (`docs/2026-10-19-conventional-override-validation.md`). This case is evidence
  on the other side of that trade, and it is the kind a held-out set should contain.

## What follows

- Two of the seven `declining` false alarms in the baseline (`Pan_flavivirus_NS5_3`
  rounds 1 and 2 @2017) now have a named mechanism, and it is in the scoring, not the
  statistics: a flat 3' penalty on a long conventional primer.
- **No scoring change is made here.** Wave 4 exists to stop thresholds being tuned on
  the 49 cases, and this case is one of them. What changes is the evidence available to
  the design that does change it.
- **It is a calibration point for `docs/bayesian-scoring-plan.md` C2**, of exactly the
  scarce kind: a within-family template with a known, specific footprint (fwd A:G@8,
  R:A@6; rev G:A@12, A:A@3) and a documented bench result (band). Add it to the bench
  corpus as a panel row once the EQA paper's panel strain can be tied to an accession,
  or as a footprint-level observation if it cannot.
- It is also the clearest argument yet for C2's smooth `w(d)` over the 5/5/rest step
  function: d = 3 is where the step function and the literature part company.
