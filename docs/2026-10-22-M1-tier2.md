## Mechanism: the discount was firing outside the 3' window

`score_primer` applied the identity multiplier at **every** mismatch position, so
`G:T = 0` zeroed a G:T wobble anywhere in the primer, not just at the 3' end. But
Kwok 1990 and Stadhouders 2010 measure the identity effect **at the 3' end**,
where it is about extension geometry; a 5'/mid G:T sits in the duplex-stability
regime, where the small position penalty already prices it and a wobble barely
destabilises. Discounting it there is unsupported — and it is what cost the
catches.

Forensics on the flipped records (distance from the 3' end; window is 5 nt,
`primer_3prime_window`):

- **Paramyxovirinae** (5,499 records changed call): **5,498 have no 3'-window
  mismatch at all** — they flip on mismatches at d = 18–26 (`rev @d26,24,22,21,19,18`),
  pure 5' region.
- **CDV_P_gene_Scagliarini** (87 changed): flips at d = 20, 17, 8; where a 3'
  mismatch co-occurs (`@d20,2`) the d2 penalty is kept and only the d20 dropped.
- **Leucocytozoon** (24 changed): mixed, a few with a 3'-window G:T.

So the dominant catch loss (Paramyxovirinae) rested entirely on internal G:T the
3'-end evidence never licensed.

## Fix: scope the discount to the 3' window

`score_primer` now applies `mispair_multiplier` only when the mismatch is within
`primer_3prime_window` (d <= w3); beyond it, position alone decides
(`assaywatch/score.py`). This matches the biology — it discounts the wobble
exactly where Kwok and Stadhouders measured it — and it keeps the bench rescues,
whose T:G mismatches are all at d <= 5. New guard test
`test_a_zeroed_mispair_outside_the_3prime_window_is_not_discounted` pins it (a
G:T at d=18 keeps the full 5'-region penalty).

The fix is right, and it is kept. It was not enough.

### Why no scope rule and no partial multiplier can save it

17 Leucocytozoon records change call between the two tables, and **every one of
them carries a mismatch at d = 2** — legitimately inside the 5-nt window, which
is why scoping does not touch them:

| transition | n |
|---|---:|
| `fail` → `detect` | 11 |
| `fail` → `reduced` | 4 |
| `reduced` → `detect` | 2 |

(detect 153 → 166, fail 120 → 105, reduced 67 → 69, no_amplicon 7,564 unchanged.)

A partial multiplier does not help either, for two reasons that compound:

- `drift.py` is explicit that "`reduced` counts as detected: it amplifies, just
  later", so moving a record from `fail` to `reduced` loses the catch exactly as
  moving it to `detect` does. Only staying at `fail` preserves it.
- At d = 2 a mismatch's base penalty is `primer_3prime_penalty: 3.0`, and
  `reduced_max: 3.0`. A multiplier `m` contributes `3.0m`, so a record with a
  co-mismatch at d = 17 (5' region, 0.5) scores `0.5 + 3.0m` and stays `fail`
  only while `m > 0.83`; one with a mid co-mismatch at d = 8 (1.0) needs
  `m > 0.67`. A multiplier of 0.8 is not a discount.

So **the G:T/T:G discount and the Leucocytozoon catch are incompatible under the
`budget` engine at any value worth having.** This is not a tuning problem to come
back to with a better number; it is measured and closed.

## Verdict

`mispair_penalty` reverts to `{}` per the rule stated when the value shipped:
*if a catch still falls, revert and record it.* What that costs, stated plainly:

- The **FAP and Barrett false fails return** (FAP 12 of 14 rest only on T:G;
  Barrett 3 of 3). The bench panels that argued for the discount are not wrong
  about those records — the tool still calls them fails, and that is still wrong.
- The conflict is now a measured fact rather than an open question: **three bench
  panels and one documented field catch disagree about the same wobble**, and the
  summed-budget scoring cannot satisfy both. Whatever resolves it has to be
  something other than a multiplier on the position penalty — the per-oligo
  verdict combination `config.yaml` already flags as the real defect is the first
  place to look.
- M1's remaining table entries are untouched and still unshipped: T:C and T:T
  stay at 1.0 for want of a bench panel, and A:G/G:A/C:C/A:A are penalty
  increases needing their own.
