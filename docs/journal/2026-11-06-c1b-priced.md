# 2026-11-06 — C1b stopped: it flags what a plain interval flags and catches none of the failures it was for

**Finding: C1b's kill tests can't be applied as written, the substitutes survive only weakly, and all 3 refused
`fails` rows stay uncaught, which was the whole reason to build it.** Stopped by maintainer decision. Nothing
shipped, and the design stays in `docs/bayesian-scoring-plan.md` as a record.

Measured on the 2026-11-01 calls with the RefSeq fixes, moment-matching mu and phi per chemistry on the assays that
clear the floor, as the plan says:

- **Kill test 1 can't be applied as written.** The posterior decides only above or below the threshold, where
  `declining` against `low coverage` comes from the change from baseline, `ever_worked` and the lineage override.
  Tested instead: its flag against the shipped Wilson flag on 148 clearing assays, 0 disagreements. Rebuilding
  `finalise` reproduced 126 of 148; the 22 misses are lineage overrides, not rerun, so that they stand is
  inference. phi is about 1, and four edge assays flip in 26-37% of bootstrap refits.
- **Kill test 2 can't be applied as written.** The plan's named cases aren't refused today (Salmonella 55/55,
  Shigella 38/38, Bd 17/17), and 35 are refused rather than 30. On today's counts it promotes 12 to a negative
  status, 6 with `works` rows dated earlier than the counts. At each row's own as-of date, none reads `declining`;
  3 read `low coverage` on 9 records or fewer, and a Jeffreys interval flags them too.
- **What it's worth.** The prior moves a refused assay little (median 14.5% of the weight, 6 records: 3/6 goes
  from 0.500 to 0.529), and it flags 13 assays where Jeffreys alone flags 12. All 3 refused `fails` rows stay
  `stable`.

The script was a one-off and isn't kept.
